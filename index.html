How to find new carrier leads

Follow these steps to build today’s call list.

1

Choose authority age

Pick Brand new (0–2 days) for the freshest MCs, or months / years, or Custom dates.

2

Set the options

Keep Still active only on to skip suspended carriers. Turn on Include brokers only if you want them.

3

Click Find Carriers

Leads Hunter pulls every matching authority from FMCSA and adds contacts, fleet and status. A week takes a few seconds.

4

Filter and call

Filter by state, fleet size, truck type or trailer. Click any MC, phone or email to copy it, and set Call status as you go.

5

Export to Excel

Click Excel or CSV to download the filtered list for your team or CRM.

How to look up a carrier

Find any carrier’s full profile in a few seconds.

1

Choose “Search by”

Leave it on Auto-detect, or pick DOT, MC, phone, email or company name.

2

Type what you have

A DOT (6960618), MC (MC463536), phone ((334) 500-0975), email, or part of the company name.

3

Click Look Up

If more than one carrier matches, you’ll get a list to pick from, with city, trucks and status.

4

Review the profile

Contacts, fleet, cargo, insurance, BOC-3 agent and any suspension warnings appear on one card.

5

Copy what you need

Click any number, name, phone or email to copy it, or use Copy all for the full summary.

A red warning means FMCSA has served a suspension notice. Check it before signing the carrier.
No match by phone or email? The carrier may have changed it. Try the company name or DOT.
Use SAFER on the card for FMCSA’s official snapshot and older history.
